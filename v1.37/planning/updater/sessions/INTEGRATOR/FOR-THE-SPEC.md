# For the specification: where v1.37's specification is wrong, unclear or silent

*The INTEGRATOR's list, for the owner. Nothing here is edited in `spec/` without the owner's word. Each row: the
section, what was found, and what the simulator does meanwhile. Those that held the work up were asked one at a time;
the rest wait for the owner.*

| # | section | found | the simulator meanwhile |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 17.10.2, 17.10.11 | "A page at 2²³ or above is UB" cannot arise: a page number is a value's 32-bit index over 512, so the index's own limit (a run past 2³² − 1, UB) is the only one a program can reach. The page rule is redundant | both checked; the page's check can never fire |
| 2 | 20, invariant 37 | as WORKER5's answer has it: the stale-line check catches only a still-cached clean line read again. The joint's checks (`cache_system.h`) now catch, where the Updater meets the cache, the first rule (an update before its page's write is answered), a direct write to a page the cache holds a line of and a direct read of one it holds a dirty line of. 23.6.11 could name them | the joint's checks counted, failing a run |
| 3 | 17.9.5, 23.6.4 | the fill port carries no request "taken" beyond its handshake, so a client that presents a request a cycle too long is served twice and the second answer reaches nobody. Nothing in the Tracer is wrong; it is a property of the port that a simulator should check | the joint's fill-port check: a response the Tracer did not take fails the run |
| 4 | 17.7, 21.5 | the RW engine's figure ("a `START` raises the first burst address on AR or AW from *t* + 3") is the RTL's at its own port. Behind the arbiter (23.11) its bursts reach `lpddr` a cycle later, and the RTL test's slave (`chip_memory_test.rs`) is no longer what answers it: the memory run's cycles are now the LPDDR model's | as 17.7 and 23.10 say |
| 5 | 30.6, 17.9.10 | "The tracer tile's result carries its last record to the master tile" does not say how: a result is a request (11.5), four 32-bit words, and the record is 68 128-bit words. Nor does 29.14 say where "the rows traced" come from: no Tracer register counts a segment's rows (`ROWS_RUN` is a run's) | the master's load names, in three of the tracer tile's header words, a place in its scratchpad (the reply's rows 4–71), a word in its data memory and one of its counters; the tracer tile's end streams the 68 words there and writes {rows traced, the Tracer's `ERRORS`, its status} to the word, both counted, before its result. The rows are the windows run times `RUN_CONFIG.rows` (17.9.3: a run traces exactly that many). 30.6 could say "with its last record written into the master tile's memory before it". **Ruled** (the owner, 2026-11-19, design notes 39): the record is pulled, not pushed; the specification now says so (30.6, 17.9.10, `SEND_END`) and the reply is 72 data words (29.14). **Built so** (stage 7): the places in the load are gone |
| 6 | 30.7, 17.6.8 | nothing tells the master that Egress has sent the reply: `EGRESS_COUNT` at 0 says its words are in the outgoing queue, and no bit says that queue is empty. A master that parks at once relies on the link to drain it | the master waits for `EGRESS_COUNT` 0, then parks; the harness runs on until the host's end has the reply's 72 words. **Ruled** (the owner, 2026-11-19): the master is done once `EGRESS_COUNT` is 0; a simulation ends when the host has every reply. As built, the count now 73 |
| 7 | 30.6, 6.5 | the specification does not say when the master answers the tracer tile's result. The tracer tile sends its result when its block is ready (30.6, step 1), before `SEND_END`; but a result's answer ends its image (`crt0.S`: the jump to the reset's PC, 6.5), after which nothing serves `SEND_END`. So the answer must wait for the block. 30.6 could say "the master answers the tracer tile's result once the block has landed; the tracer tile serves `SEND_END` while it awaits that answer" | built so (stage 7): `master.c` holds the answer and gives it after `CTR_TRACER` reaches 0; `tracer_ctl.c` sends its result itself and serves requests until the answer comes. Under v1.36's C&C 1 the answer comes at once and no `SEND_END`. **Ruled** (the owner, 2026-11-19): accepted, as built |
| 8 | 30.7 | "Then it is done": the master as built, after `EGRESS_COUNT` is 0, also takes the tracer tile's `LOAD`, which follows its result's answer (6.5), before it parks, so that its count of `LOAD`s is whole (130) for the harness. The reply is not changed by it. `SEND_END`'s op is 3, the firmware's (item 176 leaves the tracer tile's ops to it) | as said. **Ruled** (the owner, 2026-11-19): kept; the reply goes out first, and the master then goes on serving. The wait for the block's counter stays, a test owed (`OPEN-v1.37.md`) |
