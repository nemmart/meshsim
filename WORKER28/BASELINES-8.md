# BASELINES-8 — the numbers P1's latency commit moved (WORKER28)

**Before:** latency 1 (GO, READY and the error line 1 cycle, stage_delay 32): WORKER22's final B1 regression, Quest 110v
(v1.3-tracer 0d023ba), for the suites, the full-size sort and the Stage B smoke; Quest 115 (sim-idle-skip 3fd4e5f, B1 plus
stage 1) for T2. **After:** main 2d8d9c1 (stage 1 merged; GO, READY and the error line 8; stage_delay 64), Quest 118, the
full regression: every suite, the sort, the smoke and T2 **pass** (39 quick steps and 11 full steps with no failure, the sort's
checks, 64 of 64 windows in stages A and B and 4 of 4 in F for both programs, T2's 4,194,304 rows checked on each workload).
Generated by `WORKER28/tools/baseline_diff.py` (wall times masked); the summary is mine.

## Summary

- **The full-size sort, p4/uniform-k128: 1,229,008 → 1,229,687 cycles (+679, +0.06%).** Each of the sort's GO → all-READY
  round trips is 14 cycles longer (7 on GO, 7 on READY), and C&C 1's GO settle waits `go_latency` + 1.
- **T2 segment 0, C&C 3's cycles: +26 on each workload** (EVM Zone 21,141,521 → 21,141,547; Cyclone 21,365,202 →
  21,365,228): the segment's READY/GO barriers. Every other T2 figure is unchanged (the trace's rows, the drain, the
  transfers, the DMA packets, the longest replay): the tiles' work doesn't depend on the lines.
- **The Stage B smoke: unchanged**, every window in every stage: Stage B's cycles are counted from the replayer's run,
  after the boot's GO.
- **The suites:** 29 logs identical; 21 differ, each by the lines' latencies (the boot's GO → READY, the phase tables'
  starts and the totals: +7 per GO, +7 per READY, as in the accounting suite's 75,606 → 75,655 over its four phases),
  except:
  - **TracerTest: 150 → 180 checks**, WORKER30's statistics-counter tests merged between 110v and 118, not the latencies;
  - **the recipes**, where stage_delay 64 (was 32) also moves the first staged packet by 32 cycles;
  - **Sweep**: its `ready_latency` and `go/run latency` points 4 and 8 are now relative to a default of 8, so the 8
    points read +0 and the 4 points −4 (they read +7 and +3 at the old default). Changing those points (to 1 and 4,
    say) is left for whoever next touches Sweep.

Stage 1 changes none of these numbers: Quests 114-117 proved every chip's fingerprint, log and CSV the same with stage 1
off and on, at latency 1 (115) and at 8 (116b, 117).
