# spec/history

History, kept whole and unchanged. Nothing here is kept true of v1.36; where a document here and
`spec/machine-spec.md` differ, the spec is the authority.

**v1.35's specification, as it was** (moved here by v1.36, which replaced it with the RTL's):
- `machine-spec-v1.35.md`: v1.35's `spec/machine-spec.md`, the platform, in v1.35's numbering (sections 1–14).
- `replayer-v1.35.md` and `tracer-v1.35.md`: v1.35's `spec/jolt/replayer.md` (4.19) and `spec/jolt/tracer.md` (4.21).
- `CHANGES-v1.3-to-v1.35.md`: v1.35's guide from v1.3.

`spec/CHANGES-v1.35-to-v1.36.md` says where each of their sections went, and gives the name table from v1.35's
terms to v1.36's. A citation written "v1.35's 4.12" anywhere in v1.36's spec is to these files.

**What v1.35's restructuring took out of v1.3's spec,** moved whole and unchanged: each version's `CHANGES-*` guide,
the four decisions logs the spec cites (`DECISIONS-v1.2.md` to `DECISIONS-v1.3.md`), the preamble's "in brief" blocks
and history companions (`machine-spec-v1.3-preamble.md`), and section 13 as it stood
(`machine-spec-v1.3-section-13.md`). Their paths and line numbers are v1.3's. Carried text keeps its worker names
and session codes. `EDITS.md` lists every line v1.35's restructuring changed or added, with the old and new text
of each edit.
