# What v1.37 adds to v1.36

*For anyone who knows v1.36: what this folder has that v1.36 does not, and where each piece is specified.* Running
it is `RUNNING-v1.37.md`; how it was checked, with its figures, is
`../updater_wip/planning/sessions/WORKER3-REPORT.md`, and for the threshold (WORKER4)
`../updater_wip/planning/sessions/WORKER4-REPORT.md`, and for the forward count (WORKER7)
`../updater_wip/planning/sessions/WORKER7-REPORT.md`; the choices made where the documents are silent are in the
notes beside each (`../updater_wip/planning/sessions/WORKER3-NOTES.md`, `../updater_wip/planning/sessions/WORKER4-NOTES.md`,
`../updater_wip/planning/sessions/WORKER7-NOTES.md`).

**`spec/` is v1.37's specification** (its guide from v1.36: `spec/CHANGES-v1.36-to-v1.37.md`). The rows before the
INTEGRATOR's were built to the design documents of `../v1.4_wip/planning/` that it was written from:
`INGRESS-INTERFACE.md`, `UPDATER-INTERFACE.md`, `HOST-FRAMES.md` and `HOST-LINK.md`; and, for the cache's controller
and the path to LPDDR (WORKER5), `AXI-INTERFACE.md` and `CACHE-INTERFACE.md`. **The folder stands alone:** the link,
the wire and the frames' gold are copied in (`link/`, `wire/`, `pipeline/frames/gold/`; `FROM-UPDATER.sha256`), and
the rows below that name them under `../updater_wip` are the history of where they were built.

**From v1.36's machine** (as built to v1.37's specification, `spec/`, stage by stage: `../updater_wip/planning/sessions/INTEGRATOR/`):
the memory tile's peripheral 1 is the memory system and its peripheral 2 the RW engine on the arbiter (stage 1); the
line between the special tiles (stage 2); and **every chip's master tile is v1.37's** (stage 3): Ingress/Egress at
17.6's registers, with its DMA, and the Updater as its peripheral 2. `MachConfig::host_link` is gone; `MachConfig::proof`
(setting `proof`) says only that a proof comes from the host, with the Tracer and the Updater on one store. The rows
below that name `host_link` are the history of the pieces as they were built.
